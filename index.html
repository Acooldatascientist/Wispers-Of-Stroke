Hafsa Khurshid

Painter | Artist

Physicist by training, artist by calling. I am an emerging artist drawn to the beauty in life’s smallest details. My journey began with acrylics and grew into a deep love for oils. Through color and texture, I transform emotion into visual expression—painting for peace, hope, and joy, and inviting viewers to feel, reflect, and connect.
